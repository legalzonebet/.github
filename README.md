# LegalZone

**LegalZone** is an independent comparison and review platform for online gambling operators licensed.

We help users make informed decisions through transparent, up-to-date and SEO-driven content about online casinos, sportsbooks, bonuses, odds, payment methods, withdrawals and responsible gambling.

## Our platforms

### [LegalZone.es](https://legalzone.es/)

The main LegalZone platform for the Spanish market. It provides:

- Independent reviews of DGOJ-licensed online casinos and sportsbooks
- Comparisons of bonuses, promotions, odds and betting features
- Guides on payments, withdrawals, account verification and responsible gambling
- Educational content about regulated online gambling in Spain
- Analysis and research on industry topics, market trends and regulation

### [LegalZone.bet](https://legalzone.bet/)

Part of the LegalZone ecosystem, focused on comparison and affiliate-driven experiences for regulated online gambling operators around the world.

## Compliance

LegalZone does not operate gambling services, manage user funds or place bets on behalf of users. We provide independent information and comparisons about operators licensed.
